Admissions

We understand that seeking help takes courage. Our admissions team walks with you every step of the way — from the first phone call to your first day in the program.

Getting started

The admission process

Four gentle steps, each one taken together with our team.

Initial Inquiry & Consultation

Call, WhatsApp or email us. We listen, answer your questions and talk through your options in confidence.

Clinical Assessment

Our clinical team assesses your physical and mental health needs to plan safe, appropriate care.

Admission & Orientation

We welcome you, help you settle in, and introduce you to the program, the team and your community.

Treatment Planning

Together we build a personalised plan around your goals, history and healing process.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is the program for?

Empower Her is exclusively for women recovering from substance use disorders and mental health challenges, including those with co-occurring conditions.

How long is the program?

Our core residential program runs for 90 days, followed by an aftercare and relapse prevention program to support you after you return home.

Is my inquiry confidential?

Yes. Every call and inquiry is treated with confidentiality, compassion, and care.

Can my family be involved?

Yes. We offer family therapy, education and family sessions to help rebuild relationships and healthy boundaries.

Do you accept insurance?

We partner with select insurance providers, NGOs, and faith-based organisations. Contact us and we will talk through the options available to you.

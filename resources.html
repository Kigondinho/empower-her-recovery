Self-care & mindfulness

Small daily practices for a steady recovery

01

Breathe before you react

Pause for four slow breaths when cravings or strong emotions arise. Let the wave rise and pass.

02

Keep a gratitude journal

Write three things you are grateful for each evening. Small moments add up to healing.

03

Move your body gently

A walk in nature, stretching or dancing releases tension and lifts your mood.

04

Nourish yourself

Regular, balanced meals and water steady your energy and support your recovery.

05

Reach out, don’t isolate

Call a sister in recovery, a mentor or your counsellor. Connection is protection.

06

Rest is part of the work

Protect your sleep with a calm evening routine and time away from screens.

Our Programs

90 Days

Residential Treatment

Our structured residential program gives women the time, safety and care they need to heal. Our daily schedule includes:

  • Individual and group therapy sessions
  • Recovery and life skills coaching
  • Medical and psychiatric support
  • Fitness, art, and mindfulness therapy
  • Spiritual care and journaling sessions
Ongoing support

Aftercare & Relapse Prevention

Recovery continues beyond treatment. Our aftercare program provides continued counselling, support groups, and mentorship for sustained sobriety and reintegration.

  • Continued one-to-one counselling
  • Peer support groups
  • Mentorship and reintegration support
Healing together

Family Therapy & Education

We engage families in the recovery process through education, counselling, and family sessions — helping rebuild relationships and healthy boundaries.

We extend our services through community talks, awareness campaigns, and outreach to women affected by addiction and mental health issues.

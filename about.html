Our Story

“Created by women, for women — to restore dignity, healing, and hope.”

How it began

Filling a deep gap in women’s recovery care

Empower Her Recovery Centre by Nueva Esperanza was born from a vision to fill a deep gap in Kenya’s mental health and addiction treatment landscape — a space where women can heal safely, without judgment or stigma.

Founded under the umbrella of Nueva Esperanza, a leading rehabilitation provider in Kenya, we draw on years of experience in addiction treatment and mental health care. Our founders saw that women’s recovery journeys often carried unspoken pain — layers of trauma, shame, codependency, and societal pressure that demanded a gender-sensitive approach.

Empower Her was created to respond to that need.

Our approach

Recovery is not one-size-fits-all

We offer a 90-day structured residential program designed exclusively for women, blending medical treatment, therapy, coaching, and holistic wellness. Our approach integrates the medical, psychological, emotional, spiritual, and social dimensions of wellness, tailored to the needs of women.

True recovery happens when women feel seen, heard, and supported — body, mind, and spirit.

Rooted in compassion and guided by professionalism, Empower Her is more than a treatment centre — it is a sisterhood, a sanctuary, and a community of hope.

About the founder

Meet Wangui Mucara

Wangui Mucara is a passionate mental health advocate and wellness promoter with over 20 years of experience in addiction treatment, hospital management, and psychosocial care. She serves as the Clinical Director of Nueva Esperanza and Empower Her Recovery Centre, leading multidisciplinary teams in the prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation of substance use and co-occurring mental health disorders.

Wangui was among the pioneer team introducing Medically Assisted Therapy (MAT) at Mathari Teaching and Referral Hospital for the treatment of opioid addiction in Kenya. She has since worked in leading private hospitals and international NGOs, overseeing medical, nursing, and therapeutic services for individuals and families affected by addiction.

Her work is deeply personal and purposeful — rooted in the belief that every woman has the capacity to heal and live with dignity when given the right support.

At Empower Her, Wangui combines her clinical expertise with empathy, faith, and wellness coaching to create a program that honours every woman’s story and helps her rediscover her strength, identity, and voice.

When she’s not at work, Wangui enjoys nature walks, hiking, and fitness. She’s also a mother, mentor, and mental health educator committed to creating spaces of healing, hope, and holistic wellbeing.

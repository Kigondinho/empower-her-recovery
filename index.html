Who we are

A sanctuary created by women, for women

Empower Her Recovery Centre, a project by Nueva Esperanza, is Kenya’s first women-only rehabilitation centre dedicated to holistic healing, recovery, and empowerment.

We offer compassionate, structured, and evidence-based treatment programs in a serene, supportive environment where women can heal and rebuild their lives.

Our purpose

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide compassionate, gender-responsive treatment that empowers women to recover, heal, and live meaningful lives free from addiction.

Our Vision

A world where every woman has the opportunity to recover with dignity, support, and hope.

How we help

Whole-person care for body, mind & spirit

A structured 90-day residential program, supported by aftercare, family therapy and community outreach.

Residential Treatment

A 90-day program of therapy, coaching, medical support and holistic wellness.

Aftercare

Continued counselling, support groups and mentorship for lasting sobriety.

Family Therapy

Education and family sessions to rebuild relationships and healthy boundaries.

Outreach

Community talks and awareness campaigns reaching women affected by addiction.
